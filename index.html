Restore, manage or cancel

  1. Open the extension’s Settings.
  2. Select Restore purchase and sign in through ExtensionPay using your purchase email.
  3. For monthly subscriptions, use Manage subscription to manage or cancel billing.

Paid access is verified through ExtensionPay. Previously verified accounts can work offline for up to 24 hours. Restoring access does not restore locally stored poker records; import your own backup for that.

Chip results and calculations

Chip changes are derived from visible stacks and can be incomplete when a hand is joined late, a payout is missed or a top-up occurs. Review History and use the pencil to correct an amount, or the bin to delete a row. Totals and charts recalculate from recorded amounts. Chip amounts are not cash earnings. Probabilities assume unknown opponent cards are equally likely; they do not model player behavior, betting ranges or side pots.
